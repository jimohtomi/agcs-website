About Us

Avant Garde Christian School was founded in 2010 with a vision to provide quality education rooted in Christian values. It is now one of the leading educational institutions in Jos city. As a top-quality school, we use the National Curriculum mixed with an eclectic blend of other curricula from regions of the world with great academic track record. Avant Garde Curriculum also adopts a thematic approach to learning which reflects the real world and also makes learning more meaningful and more fun for your child. Our small class sizes and dedicated teachers ensure that each student receives personalized attention and support. We offer nursery, primary, and secondary education with a focus on academic excellence, character development, and spiritual growth. We are excited to have your children join us!
